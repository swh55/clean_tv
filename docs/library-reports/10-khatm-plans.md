# 10 — Khatm plans: the whole Qur'an, on the reader's own terms

**Report status:** PASS (day arithmetic unit-tested; the screens' look NOT TESTED).

## Creating one

- **Ready plans:** 30, 60, 90 days — one OK each.
- **Custom:** بالصفحات (5–40 pages a day) أو بالأجزاء (1–10 juz a day — 604/30 ≈
  20.1 pages a juz), a start delay (يبدأ اليوم حتى ١٤ يومًا), and the khatm reminder
  switch of Library settings at creation. Days and pages-per-day are computed from
  the real 604-page mushaf, never from a rounded guess.

## The plan card

One card per plan — several plans may live side by side:

- **الشكل:** «30 يومًا · نحو 20 صفحة يوميًا».
- **Progress:** the percentage big, «N من 604 صفحة · ≈ M جزءًا متبقية», and a gold
  progress bar.
- **Today:** «اليوم: الصفحات 45–65» with the mushaf place of the day's first page —
  the surah and verse it starts at, looked up from the page table (so the reader
  knows where to open).
- **Days left and the expected finish date** («بقيت 22 يومًا · الإنجاز المتوقع: 3
  كانون الأول 2026»), and, when behind, «متأخر N يومًا».

## Honesty about progress

- **Nothing is marked automatically.** The only way pages happen is the reader
  pressing «أنجزت ورد اليوم» (the day's quota) or «+5 صفحة» (extra pages actually
  read) — the brief's rule, kept to the letter: the button marks, the app never
  does, and reading in the reader never touches the plan.
- The log is history: one row per plan-day (its pages accumulate), never rewritten.

## Falling behind

The card shows the days behind (the gap between the plan's day and the pages the
log holds). **إعادة توزيع الأيام** spreads the remaining pages over a fresh count of
days at the same pace, moving the expected finish out — the elapsed days and every
logged row stay exactly as they are; nothing is deleted, nothing is rewritten.
(An honest bound: the redistribution keeps the plan's own pace rather than asking
for a new one; a faster catch-up is simply a second plan or the +N button.)

## Deletion

حذف الخطة removes the plan and its log — the button on its own card, no
confirmation gate beyond the press.
