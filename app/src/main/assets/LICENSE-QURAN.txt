The Qur'an text in this application comes from Tanzil (tanzil.net), downloaded through
api.alquran.cloud, and is used under Tanzil's notice:

  You are allowed to freely use, distribute and share the text of the Quran provided
  that you do not modify the text and include this notice in all copies of the text
  that you distribute or share. This notice applies to the Quran text only and not
  to the translations.

The text is stored verbatim, apart from two mechanical adjustments documented in
tools/quran-data/build_quran_db.py: the file's leading U+FEFF marker is dropped, and
the surah-header Bismillah that api.alquran.cloud embeds in verse 1 is split out so
the reader shows it as its own header row — the mushaf's own arrangement. The build
script gates the text's integrity (114 surahs, 6236 verses, per-surah counts, spot
checks) and records a SHA-256 of the whole text in the database's meta table.

The adhkar come from «حصن المسلم» by Sa'id ibn Ali ibn Wahf Al-Qahtani, from the
digital text of github.com/rn0x/hisn_almuslim_json, chapters «أذكار الصباح والمساء»
and «الأذكار بعد السلام من الصلاة», used verbatim with their own repetition counts
and source lines — see tools/quran-data/build_azkar.py's audit table.

The recitation audio streams from mp3quran.net's own servers (its public reciter
list); nothing is downloaded except what is asked for in the Downloads screen.
